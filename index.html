Agentic RAG over SEC Filings

An agent that answers questions about NVIDIA, AMD and Micron 10-K filings and checks whether its evidence is enough before it answers.

  • Pinecone index over 9 filings, five retrieval strategies (dense, filtered, multi-query RRF, Cohere rerank, BM25 hybrid) and a LangGraph agent that retries up to three times.
  • A simpler retrieval-ladder agent beat the diagnosis agent 0.93 to 0.64 on 14 labeled questions (single run); I traced why and added a verdict policy, CI and a 34-question benchmark. Live on Render.

Financial Fraud Detection

Two-stage fraud pipeline on 590K+ e-commerce transactions (team project, CS 6140).

  • Stage 1 predicts each transaction’s expected amount; its residual feeds Stage 2 classifiers (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, DNN, XGBoost).
  • XGBoost was best at 0.945 AUC-ROC (0.948 with the anomaly feature, a small +0.0036 average gain); SHAP explains predictions.
